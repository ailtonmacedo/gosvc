# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.

Expected behavior includes constructive feedback, technical curiosity, patience, and respect for different backgrounds and experience levels. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information.

Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when this policy is violated. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers.
